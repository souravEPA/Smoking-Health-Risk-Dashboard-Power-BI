🚬 Smoking Health Risk Analysis Dashboard | Power BI

📌 Project Overview

The Smoking Health Risk Analysis Dashboard is an interactive data visualization project built using Microsoft Power BI to explore smoking patterns and their potential relationship with health risk indicators.

The dashboard presents key patient statistics, smoking status distributions, age-group trends, and health risk indicators through interactive charts and visual elements. It is designed to make health-related data easier to understand and support data-driven exploration.

🎯 Project Objectives

Analyze the distribution of smoking status among patients.

Compare smoking patterns across gender groups.

Explore smoking duration and daily intake across different age groups.

Visualize cholesterol and hypertension risk categories.

Present key patient metrics in an interactive dashboard.

Transform health-related data into meaningful visual insights.

📊 Dashboard Features

1. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

Total Patients

Average Age

Average BMI

2. Smoking Status Analysis

Distribution of Never, Current, and Former smokers.

Percentage breakdown of smoking status.

Comparison of smoking status by gender.

3. Smoking Duration and Daily Intake

Analysis across different age groups.

Comparison of Years of Smoking (YOS) and Cigarettes Per Day (CPD).

Identification of trends and variations between age groups.

4. Health Risk Analysis

Visualization of cholesterol risk categories.

Analysis of hypertension risk categories.

Comparison of risk distributions across age groups.

5. Interactive Dashboard Navigation

Organ-specific navigation options, including Heart, Human Body, Kidney, Liver, and Lungs.

Dedicated visual space for health-related illustrations.

Clear layout designed for easy exploration of key metrics and charts.
